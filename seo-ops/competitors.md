# Competitor intelligence

First pass: 2026-11-13. This is a SERP-and-site review, not a ranking scrape. No invented positions.

## How competitors win (pattern)

Across UK, Dubai and Mumbai, ranking service sites share:

1. A dedicated **AI automation** or **AI agents** URL, not a generic “AI solutions” blurb.
2. **Use-case copy** (support, documents, CRM, lead qualification) instead of capability lists.
3. **Proof**: named case studies, Clutch/Google reviews, or a live demo agent.
4. **Commercial FAQs** (cost, timeline, stack, data residency).
5. **Local honesty**: a real office, or an explicit remote/delivery model with timezone and contract entity.
6. A working enquiry form (not `mailto:`).

Tech Eeez currently has 1–2 of these (Mumbai address, one testimonial, prerendered metadata in source). Production HTML does not even expose unique page metadata to non-JS crawlers.

## Brand SERP risk

Queries for “Tech Eeez” currently surface similarly named brands (The Techeez, Techeezy, TechTez, Techneez). Entity clarity is a GEO problem:

- Consistent legal/trading name: **Tech Eeez**
- Unique domain: techeeez.com
- NAP on contact + schema (already present in source)
- `sameAs` once LinkedIn exists
- llms.txt already states identity (good)
